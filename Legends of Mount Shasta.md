Sunrise on Mount Shasta

California's [Mount Shasta](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Shasta "Mount Shasta") has been the subject of a large number of myths and legends. In particular, it is often said there is a secret city beneath its peaks. In some stories, the city is no longer inhabited, while in others, it is inhabited by a technologically advanced society of human beings or mythical creatures.

According to the local [Klamath people](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klamath_people "Klamath people"), Mount Shasta is inhabited by the spirit chief Skell, who descended from heaven to the mountain's summit. Skell fought with the Spirit of the Below-World, [Llao](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Llao "Llao"), who resided at [Mount Mazama](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Mazama "Mount Mazama"), by throwing hot rocks and lava, probably representing the volcanic eruptions at both mountains.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legends_of_Mount_Shasta#cite_note-1) Writer [Joaquin Miller](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joaquin_Miller "Joaquin Miller") recorded various related legends in the 1870s.

Mount Shasta has also been a focus for non-Native American legends, centered on a hidden city (called Telos) of advanced beings from the lost continent of [Lemuria](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemuria "Lemuria").[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legends_of_Mount_Shasta#cite_note-lemuria-2) The legend grew from an offhand mention of Lemuria in the 1880s.[_[by whom?](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Manual_of_Style/Words_to_watch#Unsupported_attributions "Wikipedia:Manual of Style/Words to watch")_] In 1905, _[A Dweller on Two Planets](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Dweller_on_Two_Planets "A Dweller on Two Planets")_ by Frederick Spencer Oliver was published, which claimed that survivors from a sunken continent called Lemuria were living in or on Mount Shasta.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legends_of_Mount_Shasta#cite_note-3) Oliver's Lemurians lived in a complex system of tunnels beneath with jeweled walls and fur-carpeted floors within the mountain, and occasionally were seen walking the surface dressed in white robes. [[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legends_of_Mount_Shasta#cite_note-4)

In 1931, [Harvey Spencer Lewis](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Spencer_Lewis "Harvey Spencer Lewis"), using the pseudonym Wishar S[penle] Cerve,[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legends_of_Mount_Shasta#cite_note-5) wrote a book published by the [Ancient Mystical Order Rosae Crucis](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Mystical_Order_Rosae_Crucis "Ancient Mystical Order Rosae Crucis") about the hidden Lemurians of Mount Shasta that a bibliography on Mount Shasta described as "responsible for the legend's widespread popularity".[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legends_of_Mount_Shasta#cite_note-6) This belief has been incorporated into numerous new religious movements, including ["I AM" Activity](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%22I_AM%22_Activity "\"I AM\" Activity"), The Summit Lighthouse/[Church Universal and Triumphant](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_Universal_and_Triumphant "Church Universal and Triumphant")/Keepers Of The Flame,[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legends_of_Mount_Shasta#cite_note-7) [Love Has Won](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Has_Won "Love Has Won"), and [Kryon](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kryon "Kryon").[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legends_of_Mount_Shasta#cite_note-Brockman-8)

Several [UFO sightings](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFO_sightings "UFO sightings") were reported in the proximity of Mount Shasta and are speculated in local legends to be lenticular clouds hiding Lemurian motherships visiting the hidden city of Telos.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legends_of_Mount_Shasta#cite_note-9) [Bigfoot](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bigfoot "Bigfoot") sightings at Shasta have also been reported in [cryptozoology](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptozoology "Cryptozoology"),[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legends_of_Mount_Shasta#cite_note-10) as well as stories about interdimensional beings connected to the [Ascended Masters](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascended_master "Ascended master").[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legends_of_Mount_Shasta#cite_note-11)

According to [Guy Ballard](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ballard "Guy Ballard"), while hiking on Mount Shasta, he encountered a man who introduced himself as [Count of St. Germain](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Count_of_St._Germain "Count of St. Germain"), who is said to have started Ballard on the path to discovering the teachings that would become ["I AM" Activity](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%22I_AM%22_Activity "\"I AM\" Activity").[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legends_of_Mount_Shasta#cite_note-Brockman-8)
