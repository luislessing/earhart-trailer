_**Men in Black**_ (abbreviated as _**MIB**_) is a 1997 American [science fiction](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_fiction_film "Science fiction film") [action comedy film](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_comedy "Action comedy")[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-2) directed by [Barry Sonnenfeld](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Sonnenfeld "Barry Sonnenfeld"), based on a script by [Ed Solomon](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Solomon "Ed Solomon"), that adapts the [Marvel Comics](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Comics "Marvel Comics") series _[The Men in Black](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Men_in_Black_\(comics\) "The Men in Black (comics)")_ by [Lowell Cunningham](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowell_Cunningham "Lowell Cunningham"). [Tommy Lee Jones](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Lee_Jones "Tommy Lee Jones") and [Will Smith](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Smith "Will Smith") respectively star as [Agent K](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agent_K "Agent K") and [Agent J](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agent_J "Agent J"), who investigate a series of seemingly unrelated criminal incidents related to the extraterrestrials who live in secret on Earth. [Linda Fiorentino](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linda_Fiorentino "Linda Fiorentino"), [Vincent D'Onofrio](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_D'Onofrio "Vincent D'Onofrio"), and [Rip Torn](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rip_Torn "Rip Torn") also appear in supporting roles.

Development for the film began in 1992, after producers [Walter F. Parkes](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_F._Parkes "Walter F. Parkes") and [Laurie MacDonald](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurie_MacDonald "Laurie MacDonald") optioned the rights to the comic book series. Solomon was soon hired to write the screenplay; Sonnenfeld was the preferred directorial choice, which resulted in delays due to his commitments to other film projects and a failure to secure any alternative directors. [Principal photography](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography "Principal photography") began in March 1996 and lasted until that June, with filming primarily taking place in [New York City](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City"). The film's visual effects were helmed by [Industrial Light & Magic](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_Light_&_Magic "Industrial Light & Magic"). The film's [soundtrack](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black:_The_Album "Men in Black: The Album") contains the [theme song of the same name](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(song\) "Men in Black (song)"), performed by Smith, as well as the score, composed by [Danny Elfman](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Elfman "Danny Elfman").

_Men in Black_ premiered at [Pacific's Cinerama Dome](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinerama_Dome "Cinerama Dome") in [Hollywood](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood,_Los_Angeles "Hollywood, Los Angeles") on June 25, 1997, and was theatrically released in the United States on July 2 by [Sony Pictures Releasing](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures_Releasing "Sony Pictures Releasing") through its [Columbia Pictures](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_Pictures "Columbia Pictures") label. It received critical acclaim for its screenplay, humour, action sequences, and the performances and chemistry between Jones and Smith. The film was a box-office success, grossing more than $589.4 million worldwide and becoming the [third-highest-grossing film of 1997](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1997_in_film "1997 in film") as well as the [ninth-highest-grossing film of the decade](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990s_in_film "1990s in film"). It won for [Best Makeup](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Makeup_and_Hairstyling "Academy Award for Best Makeup and Hairstyling") and was also nominated for [Best Art Direction](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Production_Design "Academy Award for Best Production Design") and [Best Original Score](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Original_Score "Academy Award for Best Original Score") at the [70th Academy Awards](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/70th_Academy_Awards "70th Academy Awards"), among numerous other accolades. [A sequel](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_II "Men in Black II") was released in 2002, [a third film](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_3 "Men in Black 3") was released in 2012, and [a stand-alone sequel](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black:_International "Men in Black: International") was released in 2019, with Jones and Smith absent.

In the mid-1950s, the Men in Black (MIB) organization was founded. After secretly making [first contact](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_contact_\(science_fiction\) "First contact (science fiction)") with [extraterrestrials](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extraterrestrial_life "Extraterrestrial life") in 1961, the small, underfunded government agency becomes fully independent and secret. The MIB designates Earth as a neutral zone for alien refugees who live in secret among humans. Agents monitor alien activity and use memory-erasing [neuralyzer](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuralyzer "Neuralyzer") devices to maintain secrecy.

In 1997, MIB [Agents K](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agent_K "Agent K") and D disrupt a border patrol operation at the [Mexico–United States border](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico%E2%80%93United_States_border "Mexico–United States border") to capture a Samurian named Mikey. When Mikey becomes violent, K uses his own MIB pistol to kill him and neuralyzes the patrol officers. D, feeling too old to continue, asks K to neuralyze him so he can retire.

Soon after, [NYPD](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City_Police_Department "New York City Police Department") officer [James Darrell Edwards III](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agent_J "Agent J") apprehends a suspect, unaware that he is an alien. The alien warns of a coming threat before committing suicide. K, impressed by James' performance, recruits him into the MIB. After completing a series of tests, James becomes Agent J, and his previous identity is erased from public records.

Meanwhile, a hostile alien known as a "Bug" crash-lands in upstate New York. The Bug kills a farmer named Edgar and uses his skin as a disguise. K and J, tipped off by a [tabloid news](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabloid_journalism "Tabloid journalism") article, question Edgar's wife. They learn that the Bug has killed two aliens who were living on Earth in disguise. Their bodies, along with their pet cat, are sent to a morgue overseen by coroner Laurel Weaver.

At the morgue, the alien tells J and Laurel that "the galaxy is on [Orion's Belt](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orion's_Belt "Orion's Belt")" before dying. After neuralyzing Laurel, K identifies the alien as Rosenberg, a prince from the Arquillian Empire. K and J visit [Frank the Pug](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_the_Pug "Frank the Pug"), an alien informant, who explains that Rosenberg was protecting a miniature galaxy. The galaxy is a powerful energy source that the Bug wants to use to destroy the Arquillians. An Arquillian warship soon arrives in Earth's orbit and demands that the MIB return the galaxy, or they will destroy the Earth.

J and the Bug both realize the galaxy is on the collar of Rosenberg's cat, Orion, which is now with Laurel. The Bug captures her and swallows the galaxy. As the Arquillians prepare to destroy Earth to stop the Bug, the MIB locks down all transportation. J guesses the Bug will head to the [New York State Pavilion](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_State_Pavilion "New York State Pavilion"), where the MIB hid flying saucers during the [1964-65 World's Fair](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1964_New_York_World's_Fair "1964 New York World's Fair").

At the site, the Bug tries to escape with Laurel, but she briefly breaks free. K and J shoot down the ship, which crash-lands in [Corona Park](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flushing_Meadows-Corona_Park "Flushing Meadows-Corona Park"). The Bug sheds his human skin, revealing his true form and swallowing the agents' weapons. K allows himself to be eaten so he can retrieve his weapon from inside. J distracts the Bug until K shoots the Bug apart from within. Laurel uses J's gun to finish the Bug off.

After returning the galaxy to the Arquillians, K reveals that he was training J to take his place. J neuralyzes him so he can retire. Later, J continues his work with Laurel, who has joined the MIB as Agent L.

A long zoom-out shot reveals that the universe is contained in a marble, with which a green, gelatinous alien plays before putting the marble in its bag.

The film is loosely based on [Lowell Cunningham](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowell_Cunningham "Lowell Cunningham") and [Sandy Carruthers](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandy_Carruthers "Sandy Carruthers")'s comic book _[The Men in Black](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Men_in_Black_\(comics\) "The Men in Black (comics)")_. Producers [Walter F. Parkes](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_F._Parkes "Walter F. Parkes") and [Laurie MacDonald](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurie_MacDonald "Laurie MacDonald") optioned the rights to _The Men in Black_ in 1992, and hired [Ed Solomon](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Solomon "Ed Solomon") to write a very faithful script. Parkes and MacDonald wanted [Barry Sonnenfeld](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Sonnenfeld "Barry Sonnenfeld") as director because he had helmed the darkly humorous _[The Addams Family](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Addams_Family_\(1991_film\) "The Addams Family (1991 film)")_ and its sequel _[Addams Family Values](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Addams_Family_Values "Addams Family Values")_. However, Sonnenfeld was attached to _[Get Shorty](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Get_Shorty_\(film\) "Get Shorty (film)")_ (1995), so they instead approached [Les Mayfield](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Mayfield "Les Mayfield") (best known for his remake of _[Miracle on 34th Street](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miracle_on_34th_Street_\(1994_film\) "Miracle on 34th Street (1994 film)")_). Mayfield was briefly attached before it fell through. [John Landis](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Landis "John Landis") and [Quentin Tarantino](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino "Quentin Tarantino") were asked to direct but each declined.[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-11) As a result, _Men in Black_ was delayed, allowing Sonnenfeld to make it his next project after _Get Shorty_.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-hughes-3) [David Schwimmer](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Schwimmer "David Schwimmer") was offered the role of Agent J but he declined due to scheduling conflicts.[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-12)

Much of the initial script drafts were set underground, with locations ranging from [Kansas](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas "Kansas") to [Washington, D.C.](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C. "Washington, D.C."), and [Nevada](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nevada "Nevada"). Sonnenfeld decided to change the location to New York City, because Sonnenfeld felt New Yorkers would be tolerant of aliens who behaved oddly while disguised, terming the movie as "_[The French Connection](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_French_Connection_\(film\) "The French Connection (film)")_ with aliens". He also felt much of the city's structures resembled [flying saucers](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flying_saucer "Flying saucer") and [rocket ships](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_vehicle "Space vehicle").[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-hughes-3) One of the locations Sonnenfeld thought perfect for the movie was a giant ventilation structure for the [Brooklyn–Battery Tunnel](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn%E2%80%93Battery_Tunnel "Brooklyn–Battery Tunnel"), which became the outside of the MIB headquarters.[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-met-13)

The Brooklyn-Battery Tunnel ventilation building in New York, which was used as the setting for MIB headquarters

[Principal photography](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_photography "Principal photography") began in March 1996. Many last-minute changes ensued during production. First, the scene where James Edwards was chasing a disguised alien was to be filmed at [Lincoln Center](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Center_for_the_Performing_Arts "Lincoln Center for the Performing Arts"), but the [New York Philharmonic](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Philharmonic "New York Philharmonic") decided to charge the filmmakers for using their buildings, prompting Sonnenfeld to film the scene at the [Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solomon_R._Guggenheim_Museum "Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum") instead. Then, five months into the shoot, Sonnenfeld decided that the original ending, with a humorous [existential](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Existentialism "Existentialism") debate between Agent J and the Bug, was unexciting and lacking the action that the rest of the film had.[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-met-13) Five potential replacements were discussed. One of these had Laurel Weaver being neuralyzed and K remaining an agent.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-hughes-3) Eventually it boiled down to the Bug eating K and fighting J, replacing the [animatronic](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animatronic "Animatronic") Bug [Rick Baker](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Baker_\(makeup_artist\) "Rick Baker (makeup artist)")'s crew had developed with a [computer-generated](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-generated_imagery "Computer-generated imagery") Bug with an appearance closer to a cockroach. The whole action sequence cost an extra $4.5 million to the filmmakers.[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-met-13)

Further changes were made during post-production to simplify the plotline involving the possession of the tiny galaxy. The Arquillians would hand over the galaxy to the Baltians, ending a long war. The Bugs need to feed on the casualties and steal the galaxy to continue the war. Through changing subtitles, the images on M.I.B.'s main computer and Frank the Pug's dialogue, the Baltians were eliminated from the plot. Earth goes from being potentially destroyed in the crossfire between the two races to being possibly destroyed by the Arquillians themselves to prevent the Bugs from getting the galaxy.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-hughes-3) These changes to the plot were carried out when only two weeks remained in the film's post-production, but the film's novelization still contains the Baltians.[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-14)

### Design and visual effects

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Design and visual effects")]

Production designer [Bo Welch](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bo_Welch "Bo Welch") designed the MIB headquarters with a 1960s tone in mind, because that was when their organization was formed. He cited influences from Finnish architect [Eero Saarinen](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eero_Saarinen "Eero Saarinen"), who designed a terminal at [John F. Kennedy International Airport](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_F._Kennedy_International_Airport "John F. Kennedy International Airport"). As the arrival point of aliens on Earth, Welch felt the headquarters had to resemble an airport.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-hughes-3)

[Rick Baker](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Baker_\(makeup_artist\) "Rick Baker (makeup artist)") was approached to provide the prosthetic and animatronic aliens, many of whom would have otherworldly designs instead of looking humanoid. For example, the reveal of Gentle Rosenberg's Arquillian nature went from a man with a light under his neck's skin to a small alien hidden inside a human head. Baker would describe _Men in Black_ as the most complex production in his career, "requiring more sketches than all my previous movies together".[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-met-13)[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-15)[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-16) Baker had to have approval from both Sonnenfeld and Spielberg: "It was like, 'Steven likes the head on this one and Barry really likes the body on this one, so why don't you do a mix and match?' And I'd say, because it wouldn't make any sense". Sonnenfeld also changed a lot of the film's aesthetic during pre-production: "I started out saying aliens shouldn't be what humans perceive them to be. Why do they need eyes? So Rick did these great designs, and I'd say, 'That's great — but how do we know where he's looking?' I ended up where everyone else did, only I took three months."[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-17) The maquettes built by Baker's team were later digitized by [Industrial Light & Magic](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_Light_&_Magic "Industrial Light & Magic"), which was responsible for the visual effects and computer-generated imagery, for more mobile digital versions of the aliens.[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-met-13)

Two different soundtracks for the film were released: a [score](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_score "Film score") soundtrack featuring music composed by [Danny Elfman](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Elfman "Danny Elfman") and an [album](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black:_The_Album "Men in Black: The Album") of songs used in and inspired by the film, featuring [Will Smith](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Smith "Will Smith")'s original song "[Men in Black](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(song\) "Men in Black (song)")" based on the film's plot.

Elfman's music was called "rousing" by the _[Los Angeles Times](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles_Times "Los Angeles Times")_.[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-18) _[Variety](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_\(magazine\) "Variety (magazine)")_ called the film a technical marvel, giving special credit to "Elfman's always lively score."[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-19) Elfman was nominated for [Best Original Musical or Comedy Score](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Original_Score "Academy Award for Best Original Score") at the [70th Academy Awards](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/70th_Academy_Awards "70th Academy Awards") for his score, but lost to _[The Full Monty](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Full_Monty "The Full Monty")_.[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-20)

Elfman recalled in 2017 how he was chosen to compose the soundtrack as "strange." He was on the set of _[The Frighteners](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Frighteners "The Frighteners")_ with [Peter Jackson](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Jackson "Peter Jackson") when D'Onofrio, who was shooting _Men In Black_ on the other set, walked in and invited both men to watch him perform the final scene. Then on the way home, Elfman got a call from his agent saying that he was hired to compose the soundtrack, even though there was no discussion about it prior.[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-21)

[Elvis Presley](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_Presley "Elvis Presley")'s song "[Promised Land](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promised_Land_\(Chuck_Berry_song\) "Promised Land (Chuck Berry song)")" is featured in the scene where the MIB's car runs on the ceiling of [Queens–Midtown Tunnel](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queens%E2%80%93Midtown_Tunnel "Queens–Midtown Tunnel").[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-comm-22)

In advance of the film's theatrical release, its marketing campaign included more than 30 licensees.[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-23) [Galoob](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galoob "Galoob") was the first to license, in which they released various action figures of the film's characters and aliens.[[24]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-24) [Ray-Ban](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray-Ban "Ray-Ban") also partnered the film with a $5–10 million television campaign.[[25]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-25) Other promotional items included [Hamilton Watches](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton_Watches "Hamilton Watches")[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-26) and [Procter & Gamble](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Procter_&_Gamble "Procter & Gamble")'s [Head & Shoulders](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_&_Shoulders "Head & Shoulders") with the tagline "Keeping the Men in Black in black".[[27]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-27)

An official comic adaptation was released by [Marvel Comics](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Comics "Marvel Comics"). The film also received a [third-person shooter](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third-person_shooter "Third-person shooter") [_Men in Black_ game](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black:_The_Game "Men in Black: The Game") developed by Gigawatt Studios and published by Gremlin Interactive, which was released to lackluster reviews in October 1997 for the PC and the following year for the [PlayStation](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_\(console\) "PlayStation (console)"). Also, a very rare promotional PlayStation video game system was released in 1997 with the Men in Black logo on the CD lid. Three months after the film's release, an [animated series](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black:_The_Series "Men in Black: The Series") based on _Men in Black_, produced by [Columbia TriStar Television](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_TriStar_Television "Columbia TriStar Television") alongside [Adelaide Productions](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adelaide_Productions "Adelaide Productions") and [Amblin Television](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amblin_Television "Amblin Television"), began airing on [The WB](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_WB "The WB")'s [Kids' WB](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kids'_WB "Kids' WB") programming block, and also inspired several games. A _[Men in Black](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_In_Black:_The_Roleplaying_Game "Men In Black: The Roleplaying Game")_ role-playing game was also released in 1997 by [West End Games](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End_Games "West End Games").

_Men in Black_ was first released on [VHS](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VHS "VHS") and [LaserDisc](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LaserDisc "LaserDisc") on November 25, 1997, by [Columbia TriStar Home Video](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Pictures_Home_Entertainment "Sony Pictures Home Entertainment"). The home video release was attached to a rebate offer on a pair of Ray-Ban Predator-model sunglasses.[[28]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-28) The film was re-released in a collector's series on VHS and [DVD](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DVD "DVD") on September 5, 2000,[[29]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-29)[[30]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-30) with the DVD containing several bonus features including an interactive editing workshop for three different scenes from the film, extended storyboards, conceptual art, and a visual commentary track with Tommy Lee Jones and director Barry Sonnenfeld; an alternate two-disc Deluxe Edition was released in 2002, adding a fullscreen version and a preview for _Men In Black II_.[[31]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-31) A [Blu-ray](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blu-ray "Blu-ray") edition was released on June 17, 2008.[[32]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-32) The entire _Men in Black_ trilogy was released on [4K](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4K_resolution "4K resolution") [Ultra HD Blu-ray](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultra_HD_Blu-ray "Ultra HD Blu-ray") on December 5, 2017, in conjunction with the film's 20th anniversary.[[33]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-33)[[34]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-34)

_Men in Black_ did well at the box office. It earned $4.8 million from Tuesday night previews and went on to gross $14 million on its opening day, bringing the total gross to $18.8 million.[[35]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-35) During its opening weekend, it generated $51.1 million, making it the third-highest opening weekend of all time, behind _[Batman Forever](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_Forever "Batman Forever")_ and _[The Lost World: Jurassic Park](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lost_World:_Jurassic_Park "The Lost World: Jurassic Park")_. The film then grossed $84.1 million during its five-day Wednesday opening. It surpassed _[Independence Day](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Day_\(1996_film\) "Independence Day (1996 film)")_ for having three records. These were the biggest three-day Fourth of July opening weekend, the largest July opening weekend, and the highest opening weekend for a non-sequel film.[[36]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-MenInGreen-36) The film would hold the latter two records for three years until 2000 when _[X-Men](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-Men_\(film\) "X-Men (film)")_ took them.[[37]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-37)[[38]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-38) Two years later, its successor _[Men in Black II](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_II "Men in Black II")_ broke the record for having the highest three-day Fourth of July opening weekend.[[39]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-39) Moreover, _Men in Black_ held the record for having the highest opening weekend for an action comedy film until _[Rush Hour 2](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rush_Hour_2 "Rush Hour 2")_ surpassed it in 2001.[[40]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-40) It would remain in the number one spot at the box office for three weeks until it was beaten by _[Air Force One](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_One_\(film\) "Air Force One (film)")_.[[41]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-41)[[42]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-42) The film also competed against _[Hercules](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hercules_\(1997_film\) "Hercules (1997 film)")_ and _[Batman & Robin](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_&_Robin_\(film\) "Batman & Robin (film)")_ during its theatrical run.[[43]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-43)

_Men in Black_ grossed $250.6 million in the United States and Canada, and $338.7 million in other territories, for a worldwide total of $589.3 million.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-BOM-1) It would hold the record for being Sony's highest-grossing film until it was surpassed by _[Spider-Man](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man_\(2002_film\) "Spider-Man (2002 film)")_ five years later in 2002.[[44]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-44)[[45]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-45) The film grossed a record $10.7 million in its opening weekend in Germany, beating the record held by _Independence Day_.[[46]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-46)

Despite its grosses, writer Ed Solomon has said that Sony claims the film has never turned a profit, which is attributed to [Hollywood accounting](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_accounting "Hollywood accounting").[[47]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-47)

On review aggregation website [Rotten Tomatoes](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes"), _Men in Black_ holds an approval rating of 91% based on 92 reviews, and an average score of 7.50/10. The site's critical consensus reads, "Thanks to a smart script, spectacular set pieces, and charismatic performances from its leads, _Men in Black_ is an entirely satisfying summer blockbuster hit."[[48]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-48) On [Metacritic](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), the film has a weighted average score of 71 out of 100, based on 22 critics, indicating "generally favorable reviews".[[49]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-49) Audiences polled by [CinemaScore](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CinemaScore "CinemaScore") gave the film an average grade of "B+" on an A+ to F scale.[[50]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-50)

[Gene Siskel](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gene_Siskel "Gene Siskel") of the _[Chicago Tribune](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Tribune "Chicago Tribune")_ gave the film three-and-a-half stars out of four, praising the film as "a smart, funny and hip adventure film in a summer of car wrecks and explosions".[[51]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-51) [Roger Ebert](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Ebert "Roger Ebert") of the _[Chicago Sun-Times](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago_Sun-Times "Chicago Sun-Times")_ gave the film three stars out of four, giving particular praise to the film's self-reflective humor and Rick Baker's alien creature designs.[[52]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-52) [Janet Maslin](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_Maslin "Janet Maslin"), reviewing for _[The New York Times](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times "The New York Times")_, wrote the film "is actually a shade more deadpan and peculiar than such across-the-board marketing makes it sound. It's also extraordinarily ambitious, with all-star design and special-effects talent and a genuinely artful visual style. As with his _Addams Family_ films and _Get Shorty_, which were more overtly funny than the sneakily subtle _Men in Black_, Mr. Sonnenfeld takes offbeat genre material and makes it boldly mainstream."[[53]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-53)

Writing for _[Variety](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_\(magazine\) "Variety (magazine)")_, [Todd McCarthy](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_McCarthy "Todd McCarthy") acknowledged the film was "witty and sometimes surreal sci-fi comedy" in which he praised the visual effects, Baker's creature designs, and Elfman's musical score. However, he felt the film "doesn't manage to sustain this level of inventiveness, delight and surprise throughout the remaining two-thirds of the picture."[[54]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-54) [Owen Gleiberman](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Gleiberman "Owen Gleiberman") of _[Entertainment Weekly](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly "Entertainment Weekly")_ graded the film a C+, writing "_Men in Black_ celebrates the triumph of attitude over everything else – plausibility, passion, any sense that what we're watching actually matters. The aliens, for all their slimy visual zest, aren't particularly scary or funny (they aren't allowed to become characters), and so the joke of watching Smith and Jones crack wise in their faces quickly wears thin."[[55]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-55)

John Hartl of _[The Seattle Times](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Seattle_Times "The Seattle Times")_ claimed the film "is moderately amusing, well-constructed and mercifully short, but it fails to deliver on the zaniness of its first half." While he was complimentary of the film's first half, he concluded "somewhere around the midpoint they run out of energy and invention. Even the aliens, once they stop their shape-shifting ways and settle down to appear as themselves, begin to look familiar".[[56]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-56)

_Men in Black_ won [Best Makeup](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Makeup_and_Hairstyling "Academy Award for Best Makeup and Hairstyling") at the [70th Academy Awards](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/70th_Academy_Awards "70th Academy Awards") and was also nominated for [Best Original Musical or Comedy Score](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Original_Score "Academy Award for Best Original Score") and [Best Art Direction](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Production_Design "Academy Award for Best Production Design"), losing the latter two to _[The Full Monty](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Full_Monty "The Full Monty")_ and _[Titanic](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titanic_\(1997_film\) "Titanic (1997 film)")_, respectively.[[57]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-57) The film was also nominated for [Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Motion_Picture_%E2%80%93_Musical_or_Comedy "Golden Globe Award for Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy") at the [55th Golden Globe Awards](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/55th_Golden_Globe_Awards "55th Golden Globe Awards"), losing to _[As Good as It Gets](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/As_Good_as_It_Gets "As Good as It Gets")_.[[58]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-58)

On _[Empire](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_\(magazine\) "Empire (magazine)")_ magazine's list of the 500 Greatest Movies of All Time, "Men in Black" placed 409th.[[60]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-60) Following the film's release, [Ray-Ban](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray-Ban "Ray-Ban") stated sales of their Predator 2 sunglasses (worn by the organization to deflect neuralyzers) tripled to $5 million.[[61]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-61)

## Antecedents and influence in UFO conspiracy theories

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)&action=edit&section=18 "Edit section: Antecedents and influence in UFO conspiracy theories")]

The film drew inspiration from the 1956 book _[They Knew Too Much About Flying Saucers](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/They_Knew_Too_Much_About_Flying_Saucers "They Knew Too Much About Flying Saucers")_, which alleged UFO witnesses were being silenced by mysterious black-clad agents.[[62]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-62)[[63]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-auto-63) While prior conspiracy films had taken the perspective of conspiracy theorists, _Men in Black_ took the perspective of the conspirators. The film was the first time 'Men in Black" appeared in a major movie, cementing the Men in Black in popular culture.[[63]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-auto-63) The film 'poked fun' at government debunking, with one of the Men in Black telling a witness "The flash of light you saw was not a UFO, [swamp gas](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_%22swamp_gas%22_UFO_reports "Michigan \"swamp gas\" UFO reports") from a [weather balloon](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roswell_incident "Roswell incident") was trapped in a [thermal pocket](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1952_Washington,_D.C._UFO_incident "1952 Washington, D.C. UFO incident") and reflected the [light from Venus](app://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mantell_UFO_incident "Mantell UFO incident")"—a combination of 'official explanations' the Air Force has offered for UFO reports.[[64]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men_in_Black_\(1997_film\)#cite_note-64)
